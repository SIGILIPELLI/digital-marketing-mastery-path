# 09 · Conversion Rate Optimization Basics

**Conversion rate optimisation (CRO)** improves the share of visitors who take your desired action. Doubling a landing page's conversion rate halves your cost per lead from every channel at once — usually cheaper than doubling traffic.

## 1. Find where people drop off

1. In GA4, build a **funnel exploration** (Explore → Funnel exploration): landing page view → scroll 50% → CTA click → form submit → thank-you page.
2. Watch real sessions with a free tool like **Microsoft Clarity** (heatmaps and session recordings): where do people click, how far do they scroll, where do they rage-click or hesitate?
3. Run a quick **5-second test** with three people: what does the page offer and what should you do next?
4. Read customer messages and sales calls for repeated objections.

## 2. Form hypotheses

1. Use the format: *Because we observed [data], we believe [change] will [improve metric] for [audience].*
2. Example: *"Because 60% of mobile visitors never scroll past the hero and the CTA is below the fold, moving a WhatsApp button into the hero will increase enquiries on mobile."*
3. Prioritise with **ICE**: Impact, Confidence, Ease (score 1–10 each, multiply or average).

## 3. High-impact changes to test

1. **Message match** — the headline repeats the promise of the ad or search.
2. **Clarity** — say the price or a "starting from" price; remove jargon.
3. **Trust** — testimonials with names and photos, ratings, client logos, guarantees, GST/registration details.
4. **Friction** — fewer form fields, WhatsApp/call alternatives, autofill, no forced sign-up.
5. **Urgency and scarcity** — only when genuine (limited slots, real deadlines). Fake countdowns damage trust and can breach consumer protection guidelines on dark patterns.
6. **Speed and mobile layout** — faster pages convert better.

## 4. Test and learn

1. With enough traffic, run an **A/B test** (Level 3, Module 5) using your CMS, landing page builder or a testing tool.
2. With low traffic, make the change, compare similar periods carefully, and look for big effects; or test on high-traffic ads first (headline variants) and bring winners to the page.
3. Log every test: hypothesis, change, dates, result, decision. Learning compounds.

## Cheat sheet

| Step | Tool / method |
|---|---|
| Find drop-offs | GA4 funnel exploration |
| See behaviour | Microsoft Clarity heatmaps, recordings |
| Hypothesis | Observed → change → metric → audience |
| Prioritise | ICE scoring |
| Common wins | Message match, price clarity, trust, fewer fields |
| Record | Test log |

## How It Actually Works

Every conversion is a decision weighed between **motivation** (how much the visitor wants the outcome), **ability** (how easy the action is) and a **prompt** (a clear moment to act). CRO changes rarely create motivation — the right traffic brings that — but they remove friction and uncertainty that stop motivated people. That's why the biggest gains usually come from clarifying the offer and reducing effort, not from colour changes on buttons.
