# 05 · Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)

Meta Ads reach people based on who they are and what they're interested in — before they ever search. That makes them ideal for awareness, demand generation and retargeting. This module covers campaign structure, targeting, creative and tracking.

## 1. Set up Business tools

1. Create a **Meta Business portfolio** at business.facebook.com; add your Facebook Page and Instagram account.
2. Create an **ad account** with INR currency and your time zone (these can't be changed later).
3. Install the **Meta Pixel** on your website (Events Manager → Connect data source → Web) via your CMS integration or Google Tag Manager, and set up the **Conversions API** where your platform supports it for more reliable tracking.
4. Verify your domain and configure standard events: `Lead`, `Purchase`, `ViewContent`, `AddToCart`.

## 2. Campaign structure

1. **Campaign** — choose the objective: Awareness, Traffic, Engagement, **Leads**, App promotion or **Sales**. Pick the action you actually want; Meta optimises for it.
2. **Ad set** — audience, placements, budget, schedule and optimisation event.
3. **Ad** — the creative (image, video, carousel), primary text, headline and CTA button.
4. For lead generation you can send people to your landing page or use an **Instant Form** (opens inside Facebook/Instagram, pre-filled — more leads, sometimes lower quality; add a qualifying question).

## 3. Audiences

1. **Broad / Advantage+ audience** — give location, age and let Meta find buyers; often performs best once the pixel has data.
2. **Detailed targeting** — interests and behaviours ("Office administration", "Small business owners").
3. **Custom audiences** — website visitors (pixel), customer lists (uploaded, hashed), Instagram/Facebook engagers, video viewers.
4. **Lookalike audiences** — people similar to your best customers.
5. **Retargeting**: show a testimonial or offer to people who visited the catering page but didn't enquire in the last 30 days.

## 4. Creative that performs

1. Creative is the biggest lever. Make vertical **9:16 video** for Reels and Stories, plus 1:1 or 4:5 images for feeds.
2. Hook in the first 2 seconds, show the product in use, add captions (most watch muted), end with a clear CTA.
3. Test 3–5 creatives per ad set; let Meta's delivery favour winners. Refresh creative when frequency rises and results drop (creative fatigue).
4. Primary text: problem → solution → proof → CTA, in plain language.
5. Read results in Ads Manager: CPM, CTR (link), cost per result, frequency; judge final success by cost per lead/purchase and lead quality.

## Cheat sheet

| Level | Controls |
|---|---|
| Campaign | Objective (Leads/Sales/Traffic…) |
| Ad set | Audience, placements, budget, optimisation event |
| Ad | Creative, text, headline, CTA |
| Tracking | Pixel + Conversions API, standard events |
| Audiences | Broad, interests, custom, lookalike, retargeting |
| Creative | 9:16 video, hook in 2s, captions |

## How It Actually Works

Meta runs an auction for every ad impression and picks the ad with the highest **total value**, combining your bid, the estimated action rate (how likely this person is to take your optimisation event) and ad quality. Its delivery system learns from conversion signals sent by the pixel and Conversions API, which is why accurate event tracking — and enough conversions for an ad set to exit the **learning phase** (around 50 optimisation events per week) — strongly affects results. Broad targeting works because the model, not your interest selection, finds the people most likely to convert.
