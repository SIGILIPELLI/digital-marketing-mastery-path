# 07 · Email Automation & Segmentation

Sending the same email to everyone wastes attention. Automation sends the right message at the right moment, triggered by what people do; segmentation tailors content to who they are. Together they turn an email list into a steady source of sales.

## 1. Segment your list

1. Collect useful fields at sign-up or over time: interest (office catering vs personal orders), city, customer or prospect.
2. Use **tags** for behaviour: downloaded the menu, booked a tasting, ordered twice, inactive 90 days.
3. Common segments: new subscribers, prospects who haven't bought, first-time customers, repeat customers, high-value customers, inactive.
4. Send segment-specific content: office admins get bulk-order offers; families get weekend combos.

## 2. Build core automations

1. **Welcome series** (3–5 emails over 1–2 weeks): deliver the lead magnet, tell your story, show proof, answer objections, make an offer.
2. **Lead nurture** for enquiries that didn't book: case study, FAQ, a limited offer, a personal check-in.
3. **Post-purchase**: thank you, how to get the most from the purchase, review request after 2–3 days, cross-sell.
4. **Abandoned cart / browse** (e-commerce): reminder within an hour, then a nudge with reviews or an incentive.
5. **Win-back**: for customers inactive 60–90 days — "We miss you" with a reason to return.
6. **Birthday / anniversary** — simple and well-liked.

## 3. Build it in your email tool

1. In Mailchimp (Customer Journeys), Brevo (Automations), MailerLite (Automations) or Zoho Campaigns: choose a **trigger** (joins list, tag added, purchase, date).
2. Add **delays**, **emails** and **conditions** (if clicked / if purchased → branch).
3. Add a **goal or exit condition** so people stop receiving sales emails once they buy.
4. Test the whole flow with your own email before switching it on.

## 4. WhatsApp alongside email

1. In India, combine email with **WhatsApp Business**: broadcast lists for opted-in customers, quick replies, catalogues.
2. For automation at scale use the **WhatsApp Business Platform** (via providers like Interakt, AiSensy, WATI or Gupshup) with approved message templates and opt-in.
3. Use WhatsApp for time-sensitive, conversational messages and email for richer content; don't spam either channel.

## Cheat sheet

| Automation | Trigger | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome series | Subscribes | First purchase |
| Lead nurture | Enquiry, no booking | Booking |
| Post-purchase | Order placed | Review + repeat |
| Abandoned cart | Cart left | Recover sale |
| Win-back | Inactive 60–90 days | Return visit |
| Segments | Tags + fields | Relevant content |

## How It Actually Works

Automations perform better than one-off campaigns because they're triggered by intent: a person who just subscribed or just abandoned a cart is thinking about you right now, so relevance and timing are at their peak. Segmentation raises engagement, which in turn improves sender reputation and inbox placement for all your emails. Exit conditions matter more than they seem — continuing to pitch someone who has already bought lowers trust and raises unsubscribes.
