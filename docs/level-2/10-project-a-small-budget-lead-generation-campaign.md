# 10 · Project — A Small-Budget Lead Generation Campaign

This project combines Level 2 into one end-to-end campaign: tracking, a landing page, Google Search and Meta ads, nurture email and reporting. Run it with a small real budget (₹3,000–₹5,000 over 2–3 weeks) or build everything up to the publish button.

## 1. Brief

1. Choose an offer with a clear lead action: free trial class, catering quote, free consultation, tasting.
2. Set targets: e.g. 25 leads at ≤ ₹200 CPL within 21 days, and at least 30% of leads qualified.
3. Define what a **qualified lead** is (budget, location, timing).

## 2. Tracking first

1. GA4 with a `generate_lead` key event (Module 8), tested in DebugView.
2. UTM naming sheet for all non-Google links.
3. Google Ads conversion imported from GA4; Meta Pixel `Lead` event verified.
4. A simple lead sheet or CRM: date, name, source, qualified (Y/N), outcome.

## 3. Landing page and nurture

1. A focused landing page (Level 1, Module 3) with message match for both ad sets, proof, FAQ and a 3–4 field form plus WhatsApp.
2. Microsoft Clarity installed to watch behaviour (Module 9).
3. A three-email nurture automation for leads who don't book within 48 hours (Module 7).

## 4. Campaigns

1. **Google Search** (Module 4): one campaign, two tight ad groups, phrase/exact keywords, 15+ negatives, RSAs with assets, location targeting, ~60% of budget.
2. **Meta Leads** (Module 5): one broad ad set and one retargeting ad set, three creatives including a vertical video, ~40% of budget.
3. Optional: boost organic reach with a Google Business Profile Update and a YouTube Short (Modules 2, 6).

## 5. Optimise and report

1. Days 1–3: check tracking, search terms, delivery; add negatives.
2. Weekly: pause weak keywords/creatives, shift budget toward the lowest cost per **qualified** lead.
3. Final report (2–3 pages): spend, leads, CPL, qualified rate, cost per qualified lead by channel, best keyword, best creative, landing page conversion rate, three learnings, next-month recommendation.

## How It Actually Works

Small budgets amplify noise: a handful of conversions can swing CPL wildly, and ad platforms' learning phases need data. That's why this project emphasises clean tracking, tight targeting, negatives and qualified-lead quality over volume — decisions are only as good as the signal behind them. The habit you're building is the core of performance marketing: launch, measure against a defined goal, reallocate budget toward what works, and document why.
