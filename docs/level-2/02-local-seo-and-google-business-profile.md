# 02 · Local SEO & Google Business Profile

For businesses that serve a city or neighbourhood, the **map pack** — the three local results with a map — often gets more clicks than the website results below it. This module shows you how to win local searches like "café near me" or "CA in Andheri".

## 1. Set up Google Business Profile

1. Go to **business.google.com**, search for your business; claim it or create a new profile.
2. Choose the most accurate **primary category** (e.g. "Café", "Caterer") and relevant secondary categories — categories are one of the strongest local ranking factors.
3. Enter the exact **name** (no keyword stuffing), address (or service area if you visit customers), phone, website and hours, including holiday hours.
4. **Verify** the profile (video, phone, email or postcard depending on what Google offers).
5. Add **products/services** with prices, **attributes** (outdoor seating, wheelchair accessible), and a business description.

## 2. Photos, posts and Q&A

1. Upload real photos regularly: storefront (helps people find you), interior, products, team. Profiles with recent photos get more engagement.
2. Publish **Updates** (posts) for offers, events and new menu items.
3. Use the **messages** feature or link WhatsApp if available, and answer common questions in your description and website FAQ.

## 3. Reviews

1. Ask every happy customer for a review: share your review link (from the profile's "Ask for reviews") on bills, WhatsApp and QR cards at the counter.
2. Reply to every review — thank positive ones by name; respond calmly and helpfully to negative ones, offering to fix the issue.
3. Never buy reviews or offer rewards in exchange for positive reviews — it violates Google's policies and consumer protection rules and can get the profile suspended.
4. Review count, rating and freshness all influence both rankings and clicks.

## 4. Citations and local pages

1. **NAP consistency**: your Name, Address, Phone should be identical across the website, Google, Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Zomato/Swiggy, Practo (clinics), Facebook and other directories.
2. Put full NAP and an embedded Google map on the contact page, plus `LocalBusiness` schema.
3. If you serve several areas, create genuinely useful area pages ("Office catering in Hinjewadi" with delivery times, clients in that area, photos) — not copied pages with only the area name changed.
4. Earn local links: chambers of commerce, local news, event sponsorships, partner businesses.

## Cheat sheet

| Factor | Action |
|---|---|
| Relevance | Accurate primary + secondary categories |
| Distance | Correct address / service area |
| Prominence | Reviews, links, citations, brand mentions |
| Profile | Hours, services, photos, posts kept current |
| Reviews | Ask everyone, reply to all, never buy |
| NAP | Identical everywhere |

## How It Actually Works

Google's local results are ranked on three broad factors it describes publicly: **relevance** (how well a profile matches the search), **distance** (how far the business is from the searcher or the location in the query) and **prominence** (how well-known the business is, using reviews, links and mentions across the web). You can't change distance, so local SEO is about removing doubt on relevance (categories, services, content) and building prominence (reviews and citations) faster than nearby competitors.
