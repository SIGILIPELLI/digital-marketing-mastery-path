# 03 · Websites & Landing Pages That Convert

All your marketing traffic lands somewhere. If that page is slow, unclear or hard to act on, every rupee spent on ads and every hour spent on SEO is wasted. This module teaches you to judge and improve a website and build a focused **landing page**.

## 1. What a website must do in five seconds

1. Say **what you offer** and **for whom** — a clear headline from your value proposition.
2. Show **proof** — reviews, ratings, client logos, photos of real work.
3. Make the **next step obvious** — one primary call-to-action (CTA): "Get a catering quote", "Order now", "Book a call".
4. Load fast — test at **pagespeed.web.dev**; aim for the page to be usable within about 2–3 seconds on mobile.
5. Work perfectly on **mobile** — in India most visits are on phones. Check text size, tap targets and that the CTA is visible without scrolling.

## 2. Anatomy of a landing page

1. A landing page has **one goal** and usually no main navigation menu, so visitors aren't distracted.
2. **Headline** that matches the ad or search that brought the visitor ("Office snacks catering in Baner — hot, on time, GST invoice").
3. **Sub-headline** with the key benefit and a number: "Trusted by 40+ Pune offices · 20–100 people".
4. **Hero image** of the real product or service.
5. **Benefits** in 3–4 short points (not features alone: "arrives hot" beats "insulated boxes").
6. **Social proof** — two or three short testimonials with names and companies, star rating.
7. **Offer and price** — "From ₹120 per person", or a clear starting price.
8. **Objection handling / FAQ** — minimum order, delivery area, payment, invoices.
9. **CTA** repeated at the top and bottom — form, WhatsApp button or phone tap-to-call.

## 3. Forms and CTAs

1. Ask only what you need: name, phone, date, number of people. Every extra field lowers completions.
2. Write CTAs as outcomes: "Get my quote" beats "Submit".
3. Add a WhatsApp click-to-chat link (`https://wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX?text=Hi%20I%20need%20catering`) — many Indian customers prefer chatting to forms.
4. Show what happens next: "We reply within 30 minutes, 9am–9pm."
5. After submission, send people to a **thank-you page** — useful for confirming the lead and for conversion tracking later.

## 4. Tools to build pages without code

1. **WordPress** with a page builder (Elementor, Gutenberg blocks), **Wix**, **Shopify** (for stores) or **Google Sites** for a quick free page.
2. Use a template, then replace every section with your persona-driven content.
3. Compress images (TinyPNG or built-in optimisation) — large images are the most common cause of slow pages.
4. Install analytics before launch (covered in Module 9) so you can measure from day one.

## Cheat sheet

| Element | Good practice |
|---|---|
| Headline | Matches the ad/search; says what + for whom |
| Proof | Ratings, testimonials with names, real photos |
| CTA | One goal, outcome wording, repeated top and bottom |
| Form | 3–4 fields; WhatsApp alternative |
| Speed | Test at pagespeed.web.dev; compress images |
| Mobile | CTA visible without scrolling; readable text |
| Thank-you page | Confirms action; enables tracking |

## How It Actually Works

A visitor's decision to stay or leave happens in seconds, driven by **message match** (does this page continue the promise I clicked on?) and **cognitive load** (how hard is it to understand and act?). Ad platforms also score your landing page: Google Ads' Quality Score includes landing page experience, so a relevant, fast page directly lowers your cost per click. Removing navigation, cutting form fields and adding proof all work the same way — they reduce the effort and uncertainty standing between interest and action.

## Exercise

Audit a real website using the five-second checklist and PageSpeed Insights (record the mobile score). Then write the full content for a one-goal landing page for your persona: headline, sub-headline, four benefits, two testimonials (real ones if you have them), price/offer, four FAQs and the CTA. Build it in Google Sites, Wix or WordPress if you can.
