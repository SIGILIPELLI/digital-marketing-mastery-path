# 06 · Marketing Automation & CRM

As leads grow, spreadsheets and memory stop working. A **CRM (customer relationship management)** system keeps every contact, conversation and deal in one place, and **marketing automation** moves people through the funnel based on their behaviour. This module shows how to connect them.

## 1. Choose a CRM

1. Popular options: **HubSpot** (free CRM tier), **Zoho CRM** (strong in India, affordable), **Freshsales**, **Pipedrive**, **LeadSquared** (common in Indian education and finance).
2. Core objects: **Contacts**, **Companies**, **Deals** (opportunities with value and stage), **Activities** (calls, emails, WhatsApp, meetings).
3. Define your **pipeline stages**: New lead → Contacted → Qualified → Quote sent → Won / Lost.

## 2. Capture and route leads automatically

1. Connect website forms, Meta Instant Forms, Google Ads lead forms and WhatsApp to the CRM (native integrations or tools like Zapier, Make or Pabbly Connect).
2. Store **source and UTM** fields on every lead (hidden form fields that capture UTM values).
3. Auto-assign leads to the right salesperson and send them an instant notification.
4. Send the lead an instant confirmation (email/WhatsApp) — speed to lead strongly affects conversion.

## 3. Lead scoring and lifecycle

1. **Lifecycle stages**: Subscriber → Lead → MQL (marketing-qualified) → SQL (sales-qualified) → Customer → Advocate.
2. **Lead scoring**: add points for fit (company size, location) and behaviour (visited pricing page +10, opened 3 emails +5, requested a tasting +25); subtract for inactivity.
3. When score crosses a threshold, move to MQL and alert sales.
4. Agree the definitions with sales so marketing isn't judged on leads sales won't call.

## 4. Close the loop

1. Sales updates deal stages and outcomes in the CRM.
2. Send **offline conversions** back to ad platforms: import qualified leads and won deals into Google Ads (with the click ID) and Meta (Conversions API), so bidding optimises for real customers, not just form fills.
3. Report revenue by source and campaign, not just lead counts.
4. Automate retention: post-purchase emails, review requests, renewal or reorder reminders triggered by deal stage.

## Cheat sheet

| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pipeline | Stages from lead to won/lost |
| UTM hidden fields | Source stored on every lead |
| Speed to lead | Instant routing + response |
| Lead scoring | Fit + behaviour points |
| MQL / SQL | Marketing- / sales-qualified |
| Offline conversions | Send qualified/won back to ad platforms |

## How It Actually Works

Ad platforms optimise toward whatever conversion signal you give them. If the signal is "form submitted", they'll find people who submit forms — including time-wasters. Feeding back qualified-lead and sale events (via click IDs or hashed contact matching) teaches their models which clicks produce revenue, which can transform lead quality. The CRM is what makes that possible: it's the single source of truth connecting marketing touchpoints to sales outcomes.
