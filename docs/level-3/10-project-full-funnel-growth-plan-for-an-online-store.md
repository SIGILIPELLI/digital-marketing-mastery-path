# 10 · Project — Full-Funnel Growth Plan for an Online Store

Combine Level 3 into a growth plan for an online store that could take it from early traction to a scalable system. Use *Leaf & Kettle* (premium tea, India-wide delivery) or a real store you can access.

## 1. Situation and targets

1. Baseline (real or assumed): 8,000 monthly sessions, 1.6% conversion, AOV ₹780, 30% repeat customers in 90 days, CAC ₹650.
2. Unit economics: contribution margin per order after product, shipping, fees and returns (Module 8).
3. Six-month targets: e.g. 2× revenue with CAC ≤ ₹600 and repeat rate 40%.

## 2. Acquisition

1. **SEO**: a topic cluster (Module 2) — pillar "Indian tea guide" with 10 cluster pages; a linkable asset and outreach plan (Module 1).
2. **Paid**: Merchant Center feed improvements, Performance Max with asset groups and audience signals, brand exclusions; remarketing plan on Google and Meta (Module 3).
3. **Influencers and affiliates**: 6 creators shortlisted, one brief, affiliate terms (Module 7).

## 3. Conversion and retention

1. Product page and checkout improvements (Module 8), prioritised with ICE.
2. Two A/B tests with sample sizes and decision rules (Module 5).
3. Automations: welcome series, abandoned cart, post-purchase, replenishment, win-back; CRM fields and lead/customer scoring (Module 6).
4. AOV levers: bundles, free-shipping threshold, subscription offer.

## 4. Measurement and operations

1. Looker Studio dashboard spec: KPIs, charts, filters, data sources (Module 4).
2. AI-assisted workflows for creative and content with review steps (Module 9).
3. Monthly budget split across channels with expected CAC and the rule for reallocating.
4. A 6-month roadmap (month-by-month initiatives) and a risk list.

## How It Actually Works

Growth plans succeed when they treat the store as a system: acquisition brings customers at a cost, conversion determines how efficiently traffic becomes revenue, and retention determines how much each customer is ultimately worth. Improving any one part raises what you can afford to spend on the others. Writing explicit targets, unit economics and reallocation rules keeps the plan honest — it turns "let's try Instagram" into "Instagram must deliver customers under ₹600 CAC within 6 weeks or budget moves elsewhere".
