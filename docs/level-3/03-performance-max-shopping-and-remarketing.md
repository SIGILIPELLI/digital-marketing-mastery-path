# 03 · Performance Max, Shopping & Remarketing

Beyond search ads, Google offers automated, cross-channel campaigns and ways to reach people who already showed interest. This module explains **Performance Max**, **Shopping** campaigns for stores, and **remarketing** on Google and Meta.

## 1. Performance Max (PMax)

1. One campaign that serves across Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail and Maps, driven by your conversion goals.
2. You provide **asset groups**: headlines, descriptions, images, logos, videos, final URLs, and **audience signals** (customer lists, website visitors, interests) that guide — not restrict — targeting.
3. Requires solid conversion tracking with values; otherwise it optimises toward cheap, low-quality actions.
4. Use brand exclusions and account-level negative keywords to stop PMax from simply claiming credit for brand searches you'd win anyway.
5. Check **Insights**, asset performance and search term insights; replace low-performing assets.

## 2. Shopping campaigns (e-commerce)

1. Set up **Google Merchant Center**, verify your site and upload a **product feed** (title, description, price, availability, image, GTIN/brand, shipping). Shopify, WooCommerce and others sync feeds automatically.
2. Products appear as image ads with price in search results and the Shopping tab; free listings are also available.
3. Feed quality is your "keyword strategy": put the most important attributes in titles ("Assam CTC Tea 1 kg – Strong Breakfast Blend").
4. Fix Merchant Center disapprovals (price mismatch, missing GTIN, policy issues) quickly.
5. Run Shopping through PMax or Standard Shopping campaigns for more control over product groups.

## 3. Remarketing

1. **Audiences** from your Google tag/GA4: all visitors (30 days), product/service page viewers, cart abandoners, past purchasers.
2. Google: use these as audiences in Display, YouTube or Demand Gen campaigns, and as signals in PMax.
3. Meta: custom audiences from Pixel events, engagers and customer lists (Module 5 of Level 2).
4. Match the message to the stage: viewers of the catering page → testimonial + "Get a quote"; cart abandoners → reminder with reviews; buyers → cross-sell, or exclude them.
5. Cap frequency and exclude converters to avoid annoying people.

## 4. Demand Gen and Display

1. **Demand Gen** campaigns serve visual ads on YouTube (including Shorts), Discover and Gmail — good for awareness and retargeting with strong creative.
2. **Display** reaches websites and apps across the Google Display Network; useful for remarketing at low cost, weaker for cold prospecting.
3. Judge these channels on assisted conversions and incrementality, not last-click alone (Level 4, Module 2).

## Cheat sheet

| Campaign | Best for | Key requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Performance Max | Scaling conversions across Google | Accurate conversions + values, strong assets |
| Shopping | E-commerce products | Clean Merchant Center feed |
| Remarketing | Warm audiences | Tag/Pixel audiences, exclusions |
| Demand Gen | Visual awareness/retargeting | Video and image creative |
| Display | Cheap remarketing reach | Frequency caps |

## How It Actually Works

Automated campaigns like PMax use machine learning to predict, for every available impression across Google's inventory, the probability that this person will convert and the likely value — then bid accordingly. Your inputs (conversion definitions, values, assets, audience signals, exclusions) are the only levers, so their quality determines whether the system finds new customers or just harvests easy conversions you'd get anyway. Remarketing works because past behaviour is one of the strongest predictors of future intent.
