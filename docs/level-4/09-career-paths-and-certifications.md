# 09 · Career Paths & Certifications

Digital marketing offers many career paths — specialist, generalist, analyst, manager, freelancer, founder. This module maps the roles, the skills employers look for, useful free certifications, and how to build a portfolio that gets interviews.

## 1. Career paths

1. **Specialist tracks**: SEO specialist → SEO manager; performance marketing executive → paid media manager; content writer → content strategist; social media executive → social lead; email/CRM specialist → lifecycle manager.
2. **Analytics track**: marketing analyst → marketing data scientist / measurement lead.
3. **Generalist and leadership**: digital marketing manager → head of marketing → CMO.
4. **Product and growth**: growth marketer → growth lead; product marketing manager (positioning, launches, sales enablement).
5. **Independent**: freelancer → agency founder; or creator/consultant.

## 2. Skills employers value

1. Hard skills: SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, GA4 and tagging, spreadsheets, Looker Studio, CRM/automation, content and copywriting, basic HTML.
2. Analytical thinking: forming hypotheses, reading data, calculating CAC/ROAS/CLV.
3. Communication: writing clear briefs, reports and presentations.
4. Business understanding: unit economics, customer insight, how marketing drives revenue.
5. Emerging: AI-assisted workflows, privacy-aware measurement, video creation.

## 3. Useful certifications (mostly free)

1. **Google Skillshop** — Google Ads (Search, Display, Video, Shopping, Measurement) and **Google Analytics** certifications.
2. **Meta Blueprint** — free courses; paid Meta certification exams.
3. **HubSpot Academy** — Inbound Marketing, Content Marketing, Email Marketing, SEO.
4. **Google Digital Garage / Grow with Google** courses for foundations.
5. Certifications help you pass screening; portfolios and results win the job.

## 4. Build a portfolio and get hired

1. Include 3–5 case studies from this course's projects: problem, approach, actions, results (real or clearly labelled as practice), what you learned.
2. Show artefacts: a keyword map, a Looker Studio dashboard, ad copy and creatives, a content brief, an email flow.
3. Start real work early: help a local business or NGO, run a small budget, grow your own blog or channel.
4. Write a results-focused resume: "Reduced cost per lead from ₹420 to ₹230 in 6 weeks by restructuring Search campaigns and adding 80 negatives." Tailor it to each job description — the [Mastery Path Resume Tools](https://learnmasterypath.com/resume/) can check your ATS match for free.
5. Prepare for interviews with practical tasks: account audits, campaign plans, case questions.

## Cheat sheet

| Path | Starting role | Senior role |
|---|---|---|
| SEO | SEO executive | SEO manager / lead |
| Paid media | Performance executive | Paid media manager |
| Content | Content writer | Content strategist |
| Lifecycle | Email/CRM specialist | Lifecycle manager |
| Analytics | Marketing analyst | Measurement lead |
| Leadership | Digital marketing manager | Head of marketing / CMO |

## How It Actually Works

Hiring managers in marketing look for evidence that you can move a metric, not just describe a tool. A portfolio with clearly explained problems, decisions and results — even small ones — demonstrates thinking that certificates can't. Careers compound like campaigns: early hands-on projects build proof, proof earns better roles, and better roles bring bigger budgets and harder problems that accelerate learning.
