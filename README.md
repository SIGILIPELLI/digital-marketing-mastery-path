# Digital Marketing & SEO Mastery Path

A free, structured, module-wise digital marketing and SEO training program — entry level to master level, with step-by-step instructions, cheat sheets, and a hands-on exercise in every module.

**Live site:** https://learnmasterypath.com/digital-marketing-mastery-path/

## Contents

- **Level 1 · Entry** — Funnel, audiences, landing pages, how search works, keyword research, on-page SEO, social, email, GA4
- **Level 2 · Intermediate** — Technical SEO, local SEO, content strategy, Google Search Ads, Meta Ads, YouTube, email automation, conversion tracking, CRO
- **Level 3 · Advanced** — Link building, topic clusters, Performance Max and remarketing, Looker Studio, A/B testing, automation and CRM, influencers, e-commerce, AI tools
- **Level 4 · Master** — Strategy and budgeting, attribution, international SEO, brand positioning, growth systems, privacy and first-party data, team leadership, freelancing, career
