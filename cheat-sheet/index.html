    Digital Marketing & SEO Cheat Sheet¶

    Quick-reference tables from 36 lessons of the free Digital Marketing & SEO Mastery Path, in course order from beginner to advanced. Each section links to the full lesson with explanations and runnable examples.

    Level 1 · Entry¶

    01 · Digital Marketing Fundamentals & the Funnel¶

    Term Meaning Example
    Funnel Awareness → Consideration → Conversion → Retention 10,000 → 400 → 20
    CTR Clicks ÷ impressions 150 / 5,000 = 3%
    Conversion rate Conversions ÷ visitors 12 / 300 = 4%
    CPL Spend ÷ leads ₹3,000 / 12 = ₹250
    ROAS Revenue ÷ ad spend ₹18,000 / ₹3,000 = 6×
    CLV Value of a customer over time Regular customer vs one-time visitor
    Owned / earned / paid Website & list / reviews & shares / ads

    02 · Audiences, Personas & Customer Journeys¶

    Tool Purpose Output
    Segmentation Find groups worth targeting 3–5 segments scored
    Persona One realistic buyer Goals, pains, channels, objections
    Journey map Steps before and after purchase Touchpoints + drop-off risks
    Value proposition Why choose you One sentence + headline
    Evidence sources Make personas real Interviews, reviews, search data

    03 · Websites & Landing Pages That Convert¶

    Element Good practice
    Headline Matches the ad/search; says what + for whom
    Proof Ratings, testimonials with names, real photos
    CTA One goal, outcome wording, repeated top and bottom
    Form 3–4 fields; WhatsApp alternative
    Speed Test at pagespeed.web.dev; compress images
    Mobile CTA visible without scrolling; readable text
    Thank-you page Confirms action; enables tracking

    04 · How Search Engines Work (SEO Basics)¶

    Concept What to check
    Crawled? Sitemap submitted, pages linked internally
    Indexed? URL inspection → "URL is on Google"
    Relevant? Page matches the search intent
    Trusted? Links, reviews, brand mentions
    Performance Search Console → Performance (queries, CTR, position)
    Problems Search Console → Pages / Experience reports

    05 · Keyword Research¶

    Source Use
    Autocomplete / People also ask Real phrasing and questions
    Keyword Planner Volume ranges, bid estimates
    Search Console Queries you already rank for
    Google Trends Seasonality
    Long-tail Specific, easier, higher intent
    Keyword map One primary keyword per page

    06 · On-Page SEO¶

    Element Rule
    Title tag Primary keyword first, ~55 chars, unique
    Meta description ~155 chars, benefit + CTA
    H1 One per page, includes keyword
    H2/H3 Sections and questions
    URL Short, keyword, hyphens
    Internal links Descriptive anchor text
    Images File name + alt text + compressed
    Schema LocalBusiness, FAQ, Product; test in Rich Results Test

    07 · Social Media Marketing Basics¶

    Item Guideline
    Platforms Start with 2 where your persona is
    Bio Offer + audience + location + CTA link
    Pillars 3–5 recurring themes
    Formats Reels for reach, carousels for saves, Stories daily
    Engagement rate Interactions ÷ reach
    Cadence 3–4 posts/week + Stories
    Review Weekly insights; keep what's saved/shared

    08 · Email Marketing Basics¶

    Item Guideline
    Permission Opt-in only; consent recorded
    Lead magnet Useful freebie or discount
    Subject 30–50 chars, specific
    Preview text Second reason to open
    Body One message, one CTA, mobile-first
    Footer Address + unsubscribe
    Metrics CTR, conversions, unsubscribes, complaints

    09 · Google Analytics 4 Basics¶

    Task Where
    Measurement ID Admin → Data streams
    Live check Reports → Realtime
    Channels Acquisition → Traffic acquisition
    Top pages Engagement → Pages and screens
    Mark conversions Admin → Events → key event
    Link Search Console / Ads Admin → Product links
    Exclude own traffic Data stream → tag settings → internal traffic

    Level 2 · Intermediate¶

    01 · Technical SEO¶

    Issue Tool Fix
    Not indexed Search Console → Pages Remove noindex/robots block, add internal links
    Duplicates Canonical tags Point variants to the main URL
    Slow LCP PageSpeed Insights Compress hero image, cache, CDN
    Layout shift PageSpeed (CLS) Set image dimensions, reserve ad space
    Broken links Screaming Frog 301 redirect or update links
    Changed URL Server/CMS 301 redirect, update sitemap

    02 · Local SEO & Google Business Profile¶

    Factor Action
    Relevance Accurate primary + secondary categories
    Distance Correct address / service area
    Prominence Reviews, links, citations, brand mentions
    Profile Hours, services, photos, posts kept current
    Reviews Ask everyone, reply to all, never buy
    NAP Identical everywhere

    03 · Content Marketing Strategy¶

    Stage Content types Goal metric
    Awareness How-to guides, lists, short video Impressions, organic clicks
    Consideration Comparisons, case studies, webinars Engaged time, return visits
    Conversion Pricing, FAQs, demos, testimonials Leads, sales
    Retention Tips, newsletters, community Repeat purchases

    04 · Google Ads: Search Campaigns¶

    Setting Recommendation
    Campaign type Search, Display Network off
    Location Presence in target area
    Match types Phrase/exact first; broad with smart bidding
    Negatives Add weekly from search terms
    Ads RSA: 10–15 headlines, 4 descriptions
    Assets Sitelinks, callouts, call, location
    Bidding Max clicks/conversions → Target CPA

    05 · Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)¶

    Level Controls
    Campaign Objective (Leads/Sales/Traffic…)
    Ad set Audience, placements, budget, optimisation event
    Ad Creative, text, headline, CTA
    Tracking Pixel + Conversions API, standard events
    Audiences Broad, interests, custom, lookalike, retargeting
    Creative 9:16 video, hook in 2s, captions

    06 · YouTube & Video Marketing¶

    Element Best practice
    Title Keyword + promise, ~60 chars
    Thumbnail Face/product + 2–4 big words
    First 15s State the payoff
    Description Summary, chapters, links
    Captions Always; correct auto captions
    Analytics CTR, avg view duration, retention
    Shorts Vertical discovery; repurpose highlights

    07 · Email Automation & Segmentation¶

    Automation Trigger Goal
    Welcome series Subscribes First purchase
    Lead nurture Enquiry, no booking Booking
    Post-purchase Order placed Review + repeat
    Abandoned cart Cart left Recover sale
    Win-back Inactive 60–90 days Return visit
    Segments Tags + fields Relevant content

    08 · Conversion Tracking: GA4 Events & UTMs¶

    Parameter Example
    utm_source instagram, newsletter, google
    utm_medium social, email, cpc, qr
    utm_campaign diwali_catering
    utm_content reel_v2, header_link
    Key event generate_lead, whatsapp_click, purchase
    Testing DebugView, Tag Manager Preview

    09 · Conversion Rate Optimization Basics¶

    Step Tool / method
    Find drop-offs GA4 funnel exploration
    See behaviour Microsoft Clarity heatmaps, recordings
    Hypothesis Observed → change → metric → audience
    Prioritise ICE scoring
    Common wins Message match, price clarity, trust, fewer fields
    Record Test log

    Level 3 · Advanced¶

    01 · Link Building & Digital PR¶

    Tactic Effort Value
    Original data study High Very high
    Tools/templates Medium High
    Journalist requests Medium High
    Unlinked mentions Low Medium
    Broken link building Medium Medium
    Local partnerships Low–medium Medium (local)
    Paid links / PBNs — Risky, violates policy

    02 · Topic Clusters & Content at Scale¶

    Element Purpose
    Pillar page Broad, comprehensive hub
    Cluster page Deep answer to one subtopic
    Internal links Cluster ↔ pillar, descriptive anchors
    SERP overlap test Same results → same page
    Content brief Consistent quality at scale
    Audit Update, merge, prune quarterly

    03 · Performance Max, Shopping & Remarketing¶

    Campaign Best for Key requirement
    Performance Max Scaling conversions across Google Accurate conversions + values, strong assets
    Shopping E-commerce products Clean Merchant Center feed
    Remarketing Warm audiences Tag/Pixel audiences, exclusions
    Demand Gen Visual awareness/retargeting Video and image creative
    Display Cheap remarketing reach Frequency caps

    04 · Marketing Dashboards with Looker Studio¶

    Component Use
    Scorecard + comparison KPI and change vs previous period
    Time series Trends over weeks
    Bar chart Compare channels
    Table Campaign/page detail
    Calculated field CPL, ROAS, conversion rate
    Controls Date range, filters
    Schedule delivery Automatic emailed reports

    05 · A/B Testing & Experimentation¶

    Principle Rule
    One primary metric Decided before launch
    Concurrent random split A and B at the same time
    Sample size Calculate in advance
    Duration ≥ 1–2 full weeks
    No peeking Stop at planned sample
    Significance ~95% and check interval
    Log Record every test and learning

    06 · Marketing Automation & CRM¶

    Concept Meaning
    Pipeline Stages from lead to won/lost
    UTM hidden fields Source stored on every lead
    Speed to lead Instant routing + response
    Lead scoring Fit + behaviour points
    MQL / SQL Marketing- / sales-qualified
    Offline conversions Send qualified/won back to ad platforms

    07 · Influencer & Affiliate Marketing¶

    Item Guideline
    Creator fit Audience match + engagement, not just followers
    Brief Goal, message, deliverables, approval
    Usage rights Agree partnership ads/whitelisting
    Disclosure #ad / Paid partnership upfront (ASCI)
    Tracking Unique codes + UTM links
    Affiliates Commission, cookie window, banned tactics

    08 · E-commerce Marketing¶

    Lever Metric it moves
    Better product pages Conversion rate
    Bundles, free-shipping threshold AOV
    Post-purchase flows, subscriptions Repeat rate, CLV
    Shopping/PMax, marketplace ads New customers (CAC)
    Reviews with photos Conversion rate
    Contribution margin True profitability

    09 · AI Tools for Marketers¶

    Task AI use Human role
    Keyword clustering Group by intent Validate with SERPs
    Ad copy Generate variations Pick, edit, test
    Content Outline, draft Add expertise, fact-check
    Creative Resize, edit, captions Brand check
    Analysis Explain data Verify, decide
    Chatbots FAQs, lead capture Escalation, review

    Level 4 · Master¶

    01 · Marketing Strategy & Budgeting¶

    Step Output
    Business → marketing targets Leads, customers, revenue by month
    Constraint Awareness, conversion or retention
    Strategy Segments, positioning, choices, channel roles
    Budget Top-down vs bottom-up + test budget
    Allowable CAC CLV ÷ target ratio
    Governance Monthly review + reallocation rules

    02 · Attribution & Marketing Mix Modelling¶

    Method Best for Weakness
    Last click Simple reporting Over-credits bottom funnel
    Data-driven Daily optimisation Only trackable touches
    Geo / lift tests True incrementality Needs scale, time
    MMM Budget allocation, offline Needs data, modelling skill
    Triangulation Strategic decisions More effort

    03 · SEO at Scale & International SEO¶

    Problem Solution
    Thin templated pages Unique data, quality thresholds, noindex empties
    Faceted URL explosion Canonicals, noindex, parameter control
    Wasted crawling Log analysis, clean sitemaps
    Multiple countries ccTLD / subfolder + hreflang
    Multiple languages Native localisation, local keywords
    Big changes Migration playbook + monitoring

    04 · Brand Positioning & Messaging¶

    Element Output
    Positioning Target, category, alternatives, attributes, value
    Brand promise One line
    Message pillars 3–4 with proof points
    Tone of voice Traits + do/don't words
    Identity Logo, colours, type, imagery
    Brand metrics Branded search, direct traffic, share of voice

    05 · Growth Marketing Systems¶

    Concept Purpose
    North star metric One measure of customer value
    Input metrics Levers teams own
    AARRR Lifecycle map
    Cohort analysis Retention by sign-up period
    ICE / RICE Prioritise experiments
    Growth loop Compounding acquisition
    Weekly cadence Review → decide → launch

    06 · Privacy, Consent & First-Party Data¶

    Area Practice
    Consent Specific, informed, easy to withdraw
    Notice Plain-language privacy policy
    Minimise Collect only what's needed
    Tags CMP + Consent Mode where required
    Signals Conversions API, enhanced conversions
    First-party data Value exchange + CRM/CDP with consent

    07 · Leading a Marketing Team or Agency¶

    Area Practice
    Structure Generalist → channel specialists → goal-aligned teams
    In-house vs outsource Keep strategy, brand, data in-house
    Hiring Scorecards + practical tests
    Agencies Clear brief, you own accounts, KPI reviews
    Goals OKRs tied to business goals
    Rhythm Weekly team, monthly business review

    08 · Freelancing & Client Management¶

    Area Practice
    Niche Service or industry specialisation
    Offers Packaged, clear deliverables
    Leads Network, LinkedIn, mini-audits, platforms
    Pricing Value-based; retainers for stability
    Contract Scope, payment, ownership, termination
    Tax GST registration when required; CA advice
    Retention Expectations, monthly reports, proactive updates

    09 · Career Paths & Certifications¶

    Path Starting role Senior role
    SEO SEO executive SEO manager / lead
    Paid media Performance executive Paid media manager
    Content Content writer Content strategist
    Lifecycle Email/CRM specialist Lifecycle manager
    Analytics Marketing analyst Measurement lead
    Leadership Digital marketing manager Head of marketing / CMO
