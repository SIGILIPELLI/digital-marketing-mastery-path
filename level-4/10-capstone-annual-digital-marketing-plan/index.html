    10 · Capstone — Annual Digital Marketing Plan¶

    Your capstone is a complete annual digital marketing plan for a real business (ideally one you can work with) or a detailed realistic case. It should be strong enough to present to a founder, a hiring panel or a client — and to execute.

    1. Diagnosis¶

    1. Business overview, goals and revenue targets for the year.
    2. Market and competitor analysis; SWOT.
    3. Current performance baseline across channels (traffic, leads, CAC, conversion, retention) from GA4, Search Console, ad accounts and CRM.
    4. The core constraint (awareness, conversion or retention) with evidence.

    2. Strategy¶

    1. Target segments, personas and positioning statement; brand promise and message pillars (Module 4).
    2. Annual objectives and a north star metric with input metrics (Module 5).
    3. Channel roles and strategic choices, including what you won't do (Module 1).

    3. Plans by area¶

    1. SEO and content: technical priorities, topic clusters, link-building assets, international/scale considerations if relevant.
    2. Paid media: Search, Shopping/PMax, Meta, YouTube — structure, audiences, creative plan.
    3. Lifecycle: email/WhatsApp automations, CRM pipeline, lead scoring, retention and referral programmes.
    4. Brand and social: content pillars, influencer partnerships, community.
    5. CRO and experimentation: test roadmap with prioritisation.

    4. Budget, measurement and governance¶

    1. Annual budget by quarter and channel (top-down and bottom-up), test budget and reallocation rules (Module 1).
    2. Measurement plan: conversion definitions, tracking (consent-aware, first-party), dashboard spec, attribution approach and one incrementality test (Modules 2, 6).
    3. Team and partners: roles, agency/freelancer scope, operating rhythm and OKRs (Module 7).
    4. Quarterly roadmap and risk register (e.g. platform policy changes, seasonality, budget cuts).

    Deliverable checklist¶

    Section Done?
    Diagnosis with baseline and constraint
    Positioning and messaging
    Objectives, north star, inputs
    Channel plans (SEO, paid, lifecycle, brand)
    Experiment roadmap
    Budget + reallocation rules
    Measurement + incrementality test
    Team, rhythm, OKRs, roadmap, risks

    How It Actually Works¶

    An annual plan is a set of bets with explicit assumptions — about audiences, channels, costs and conversion — organised so you can tell quickly which bets are paying off. Its real value appears in execution: monthly reviews against the plan, disciplined reallocation and a learning log turn a static document into a system that improves through the year. That ability — to plan, measure and adapt — is what distinguishes a marketing leader from a channel operator.

    Exercise¶

    Produce the full plan (15–25 slides or a 10–15 page document) using the checklist, and present it in 15 minutes to a peer, mentor or business owner. Collect their three toughest questions and add answers as an appendix. This capstone is your strongest portfolio piece — include it in your job applications and client pitches.
